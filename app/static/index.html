i How does this workflow work — and how is it measured?Follow an imaging order from the modality worklist through acquisition to archive verification, and see the statistics used. Show ＋
01 · SCHEDULECreate or receive an order

Use New order or let the HIS send one (HL7 ORM/ADT). Modalities retrieve scheduled steps with DICOM Modality Worklist (C-FIND), filtered by their station AE title and modality.

02 · ACQUIREWatch the exam progress

Modalities report started and completed exams through MPPS. Start or complete a step by hand when a modality cannot; image arrival also closes a scheduled step.

03 · VERIFYConfirm archive commitment

A modality asks whether its images are safely stored. PAXS verifies every referenced instance and answers with an N-EVENT-REPORT. Failed requests show the missing instances.

04 · MEASUREStatistics used

Proportions carry Wilson score intervals; means carry Student-t intervals. Daily volume is tracked on an individuals (I-MR) control chart with Western Electric rules; trend is an OLS fit with a prediction band. Histograms use Freedman–Diaconis bins and a Gaussian KDE (Silverman bandwidth); outliers are Tukey fences (1.5 × IQR). Queue health uses Little's law, L = λW.

05 · AUDITDICOM data quality

Each record is checked against DICOM value-representation limits (SH ≤ 16, AE ≤ 16, LO ≤ 64, PN components), Defined Terms for Modality (0008,0060), duplicate accessions, stale steps, unscheduled acquisitions and unanswered commitments.
